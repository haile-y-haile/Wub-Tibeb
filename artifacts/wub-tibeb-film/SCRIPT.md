- Promise: The holiday kemis you wore once can become someone else's festival dress, and theirs can become yours. Dress for dress, no money.
- Identity: ivory #f6efe0, forest #1f4a36, restrained gold #c9a14a, clay accent #b04a33 (from the app's tibeb band). Fraunces display, Figtree body, Wub Ethiopic / Noto Sans Ethiopic for Amharic.
- Hero: the existing demonstration kemis photographs (kemis-1..6) and the woven tibeb border.

- Arc: problem (quick holiday snaps), then exchange (wide, rhythmic), then mechanism (match, chat, confirm), then tagline burst and a calm lockup.
- Payoff: dresses circle into the logo's diamond. The lockup is the only centred, still, logo-led frame.
