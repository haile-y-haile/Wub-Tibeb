---
name: Handoff disputes
description: Why disputed physical exchanges must not automatically release garments or count as completed.
---

After either participant confirms physical handoff, blocking or hiding must not automatically make either garment available again. Moderator closure archives both garments and preserves confirmations/history; it is not bilateral completion.

**Why:** A garment may already have changed hands. A block, report, or cancellation is not evidence that it was returned. Relisting it could offer a garment the original member no longer possesses.

**How to apply:** Preserve this distinction in future cancellation, account-deletion, moderation, and history changes. Only both participants' confirmations can count a swap as completed.
