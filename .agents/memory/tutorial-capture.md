---
name: Tutorial capture boundaries
description: Privacy and accuracy constraints for on-site instructional recordings.
---

Keep how-to recording sessions isolated from live member activity. Use the actual listing form where feasible, and visibly label illustrative matches, messages and exchanges as examples rather than completed real transactions.

**Why:** First-time-user guides need to explain the actual workflow without creating unwanted listings or matches, exposing private member conversations, or suggesting that a demonstration performed a real exchange.

**How to apply:** When refreshing tutorials, check the current app's labels and consent steps before recording. Do not use production member data or bypass authentication to obtain instructional screenshots. Clearly distinguish illustrative screens from real component capture in any claims about the footage.
